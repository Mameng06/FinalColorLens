## SECTION 9: TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS (12:30 - 14:00)

### Step 11: Tips for Accurate Color Detection

**[SCREEN: Show tips visually]**

**NARRATOR:**
"Here are some tips to get the best results from ColorLens:"

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip one: Use good lighting. Natural daylight or bright indoor lighting works best. Avoid very dark environments."

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip two: Hold your phone steady. Keep the camera still while measuring to get accurate readings. If needed, use the Freeze Frame feature to capture a still image."

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip three: Position the crosshair precisely. Make sure the red dot in the center is pointing directly at the color you want to identify, not at the edge or a different colored area."

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip four: Enable voice mode. If you're colorblind, having colors spoken aloud makes the app much more accessible and useful. You don't need to look at the screen to know the color."

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip five: Check the confidence percentage. Higher percentages, like 90% or above, mean the app is very confident about the color. Lower percentages might mean the lighting or surface isn't ideal."

**NARRATOR:**
"Tip six: Use Freeze Frame for precision. If you're having trouble holding the phone steady, freeze the frame to get a more accurate reading."

## SECTION 11: TROUBLESHOOTING (15:30 - 17:00)

### Step 13: Common Issues and Solutions

**[SCREEN: Show troubleshooting scenarios]**

**NARRATOR:**
"If you encounter any issues, here are some solutions:"

**NARRATOR:**
"Issue one: Colors seem inaccurate. Solution: Make sure you have good lighting - natural daylight or bright indoor lights work best. Also ensure the crosshair is pointing directly at the color you want to identify, not at a mixed or shadowed area."

**NARRATOR:**
"Issue two: Voice isn't working. Solution: Check your phone's volume settings, and make sure voice mode is enabled in the app settings. Also verify that Voice Mode is not set to 'Disable'."

**NARRATOR:**
"Issue three: Camera permission denied. Solution: Go to your phone's settings, find ColorLens, and enable camera permissions."

**NARRATOR:**
"Issue four: The app isn't detecting colors. Solution: Make sure the crosshair is positioned over a colored area, hold the phone steady, and ensure good lighting. Try using Freeze Frame if you're having trouble keeping the phone still."

**NARRATOR:**
"Issue five: Low confidence percentages. Solution: Move to better lighting, make sure the crosshair is pointing at a solid colored area (not a pattern or gradient), and hold the phone steady."
